# Aerospace Calculations (Reference)

## Atmosphere (ISA, troposphere)

- Temperature: $T = T_0 + L h$
- Pressure: $P = P_0 \left(\frac{T}{T_0}\right)^{-g/(L R)}$
- Density: $\rho = \frac{P}{R T}$

## Dynamic pressure

- $q = \tfrac{1}{2} \rho V^2$

## Lift / Drag

- $L = q S C_L$
- $D = q S C_D$
- $C_D = C_{D0} + k \alpha^2$ (simple parabolic)

## Wing loading

- $W/S = \frac{W}{S}$

## Thrust-to-weight

- $T/W = \frac{T}{W}$

## Glide ratio

- $L/D = \frac{C_L}{C_D}$

## Range / Endurance (energy-limited, simplified)

- $E_{usable} = e_{spec} \cdot m \cdot \eta$
- $P_{req} \approx W V C_{D,eff}$
- Endurance: $t = \frac{E_{usable}}{P_{req}}$
- Range: $R = V t$
