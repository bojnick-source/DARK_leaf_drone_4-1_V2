# Kite Power Calculations (Reference)

## Aerodynamic Forces

- $q = \tfrac{1}{2} \rho V^2$
- $L = q S C_L$
- $D = q S C_D$

## Tether Tension

- $T = \sqrt{L^2 + D^2}$

## Mechanical Power

- $P_{mech} = T \cdot v_{reel}$

## Electrical Power

- $P_{elec} = P_{mech} \cdot \eta_{tether}$

## Heuristic Reel Speed

- $v_{reel} \approx k V_{wind}$ (typically $k \in [0.1, 0.5]$)
