# General Mathematics Reference (Broad Coverage)

This file complements mathlib by listing broad mathematical areas relevant to advanced computational engineering. It is intentionally high-level and non-exhaustive, acting as a jump-table for AI reasoning.

## Core Analysis
- Linear algebra (matrix factorization, eigenvalues, SVD)
- Numerical methods (root finding, integration, differentiation)
- Optimization (convex, constrained, stochastic)
- Probability & statistics (distributions, inference, uncertainty)
- PDE/ODEs (time-stepping, stability, discretization)

## Geometry & Mechanics
- Differential geometry (curvature, surfaces)
- Rigid body dynamics (inertia, rotation matrices, quaternions)
- Continuum mechanics (stress/strain tensors)
- Elasticity/plasticity (constitutive models)

## Control & Estimation
- State-space systems
- Kalman filters / observers
- Robust control (H∞, μ-synthesis)
- Optimal control (LQR, MPC)

## Energy & Thermodynamics
- Heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation)
- Thermodynamic cycles
- Exergy and efficiency metrics

## Fluids & Aero
- Navier–Stokes approximations
- Compressible flow, Mach relations
- Turbulence models (RANS/LES concepts)

## Electromagnetics
- Circuit theory (AC/DC, impedance)
- Maxwell equations (field interactions)
- Power electronics (switching models)

## Materials & Structures
- Fracture mechanics (stress intensity)
- Buckling/instability
- Fatigue and damage accumulation

## Signal & Information
- Fourier/Laplace transforms
- Spectral analysis
- Information theory (entropy, mutual information)

## Computing Methods
- Monte Carlo methods
- Surrogate modeling / response surfaces
- Sensitivity analysis
