# Wind Ship Calculations (Reference)

## Apparent Wind

- $\vec{V}_{app} = \vec{V}_{true} - \vec{V}_{ship}$

## Sail Aerodynamic Force

- $L = \tfrac{1}{2} \rho_{air} V_{app}^2 S C_L$
- $D = \tfrac{1}{2} \rho_{air} V_{app}^2 S C_D$

## Hull Drag (simple)

- $D_h \approx \tfrac{1}{2} \rho_{water} V^2 S_{wet} C_f$

## Net Drive Force

- $F_{drive} = F_{sail} \cos(\beta)$
- $F_{net} = F_{drive} - D_h$

## Propulsive Efficiency

- $\eta = \frac{F_{drive}}{F_{sail}}$
