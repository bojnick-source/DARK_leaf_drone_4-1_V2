# Circuits Calculations (Reference)

## Ohm’s Law

- $V = I R$

## Power

- $P = V I = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$

## Capacitor (AC)

- $X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C}$
- Admittance: $Y_C = j \omega C$

## Inductor (AC)

- $X_L = \omega L$
- Admittance: $Y_L = \frac{1}{j \omega L}$

## Nodal Analysis (DC/AC)

- Build admittance matrix $G$ and solve $G \cdot V = I$

## RC Time Constant

- $\tau = R C$

## RLC Natural Frequency

- $\omega_n = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L C}}$
- Damping (series): $\zeta = \frac{R}{2} \sqrt{\frac{C}{L}}$
