# Sales & Shipping Calculations (Reference)

## Revenue

- $R = P_{unit} (1 - d) \cdot N$

## Cost

- $C = C_{unit} \cdot N$

## Gross Margin

- $M = R - C$
- Margin rate: $\frac{M}{R}$

## Shipping Cost

- $C_{ship} = d_{km} \cdot m_{kg} \cdot r_{usd/(kg\cdot km)} + fee$

## Net Profit (simplified)

- $\Pi = M - C_{ship}$
